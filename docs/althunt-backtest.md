Generated by `scripts/althunt.py` on 2026-10-26 11:59 UTC. Train = hours before 2025-07-01 (thresholds chosen there), test = 2025-07-01 .. 2026-09-30 (reported, never tuned). Universe: 683 Binance USD-M USDT alt perps that traded in the window (delisted ones included; BTC, ETH, stable, index and tradfi perps excluded); OI / long-short detectors on a random sample of 150 coins (seed 7). Cache: 869 MB in 48,502 archive files under `~/Library/Caches/Depth/althunt`.

Play target: max move from the firing hour's close of >= 30% within 24h in the play's direction (up for pump / squeeze detectors, down for top / dump detectors, either for regime detectors). Precision is the share of firings that reached it; *base* is the rate over all coin-hours of the same population and period; *exp* is the volatility-matched base rate (same 7d realized-vol bucket as each firing), so lift_vm > 1 means the detector knows more than "this coin is volatile". A firing is a true state >= 24h after that detector's previous firing on the coin. Intervals are Wilson 95%.

Verdict rule (test period only): SHIP = >= 20 firings on >= 5 coins, Wilson lower bound above the vol-matched base rate, and precision above the best naive baseline on the same target (one-sided two-proportion z >= 1.645). Naive baselines: chase the 24h gainer, a 1h volume spike, or (down) anything that ran up over 7 days; each baseline's threshold is chosen on train like the detectors'.
